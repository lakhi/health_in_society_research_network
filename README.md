# Health in Society Research Network

## Overview
The Health in Society Research Network aims to facilitate research and collaboration in the field of public health and societal well-being at the Univeristy of Vienna. This project develops visualisations that reflect the research interests and collaborations of the members across universities in Austria and around the world.

## Visualisations
- Word Cloud based on the health research keywords and methods employed by the members
- Maps based on collaborations of the health research network
- Alluvial Plots/Sankey diagrams on the collaborations between researchers across Austria and the world
